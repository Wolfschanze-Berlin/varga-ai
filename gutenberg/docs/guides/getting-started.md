# Getting Started with Varga AI

Welcome to Varga AI! This guide will help you set up your account and start automating your business processes with pre-built AI agents.

## Account Setup

### 1. Sign Up for Your Account

1. Visit [app.varga-ai.com](https://app.varga-ai.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" and choose your plan:
   - **Starter**: $29/month - 5 agents, 1,000 tasks/month
   - **Professional**: $99/month - 15 agents, 5,000 tasks/month
   - **Enterprise**: Custom pricing - Unlimited agents and tasks

3. Complete the onboarding wizard:
   - Business information
   - Primary use cases
   - Integration preferences

### 2. Connect Your Business Tools

During setup, connect your existing business tools:

- **Email**: Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP
- **Communication**: Slack, Microsoft Teams
- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- **Accounting**: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks
- **E-commerce**: Shopify, WooCommerce

## Your First Agent

### Step 1: Browse the Agent Marketplace

1. Navigate to **Marketplace** in your dashboard
2. Explore 25+ pre-built agents across 5 categories:
   - **Sales**: Lead qualification, appointment scheduling
   - **Customer Service**: Ticket handling, FAQ responses
   - **Operations**: Inventory tracking, order processing
   - **Finance**: Invoice processing, payment reminders
   - **Marketing**: Content creation, social media scheduling

### Step 2: Activate Your First Agent

Let's set up a **Customer Service Bot** as your first agent:

1. Click on "FAQ Responder" in the Customer Service category
2. Review the agent capabilities:
   - Automatically responds to common customer questions
   - Escalates complex issues to human agents
   - Integrates with your help desk system

3. Click **"Activate Agent"**

### Step 3: Configure the Agent

1. **Basic Settings**:
   - Agent name: "Customer Support Bot"
   - Response tone: Professional, Friendly, or Casual
   - Operating hours: 9 AM - 6 PM EST

2. **Knowledge Base**:
   - Upload your FAQ documents
   - Connect to your help center
   - Add product information

3. **Integrations**:
   - Connect to your email system
   - Link to your CRM for context
   - Set up Slack notifications

4. **Escalation Rules**:
   - When to transfer to human agents
   - Priority levels for different issue types
   - Notification preferences

### Step 4: Test Your Agent

1. Use the built-in **Test Console** to simulate customer interactions
2. Try various question types:
   - "What are your business hours?"
   - "How do I return a product?"
   - "I need help with my account"

3. Review responses and adjust settings if needed

## Creating Your First Workflow

### What are Workflows?

Workflows chain multiple agents together to automate complex business processes. For example: Lead → Qualification → Follow-up Email → CRM Update.

### Build a Simple Sales Workflow

1. Go to **Workflows** > **Create New**
2. Choose template: "Lead to Customer Journey"
3. Configure the workflow steps:

   **Step 1**: Lead Qualifier Agent
   - Analyzes incoming leads
   - Scores lead quality (1-10)
   - Determines next action

   **Step 2**: Appointment Scheduler Agent
   - Books meetings for high-quality leads
   - Sends calendar invites
   - Sets reminders

   **Step 3**: Follow-up Email Agent
   - Sends personalized follow-up emails
   - Schedules additional touchpoints
   - Updates CRM records

4. Set triggers and conditions:
   - Trigger: New lead in CRM
   - Condition: Lead score > 7
   - Action: Start workflow

## Best Practices

### 1. Start Small
- Begin with 1-2 agents in your highest-impact areas
- Master those before expanding to other use cases
- Focus on processes that are repetitive and rule-based

### 2. Proper Configuration
- Invest time in initial setup and training
- Regularly update knowledge bases and rules
- Monitor performance and adjust settings

### 3. Human-in-the-Loop
- Always include escalation paths to human agents
- Set clear boundaries for agent capabilities
- Review agent decisions periodically

### 4. Integration Hygiene
- Keep your connected tools up-to-date
- Monitor integration health regularly
- Test workflows after any system changes
