```

This quick assessment will help me:
- Choose appropriate vocabulary and grammar
- Create suitable exercises
- Set realistic goals
- Provide the right level of challenge

**Don't worry about being perfect - just do your best!**

Are you ready to begin? Type "yes" when you're ready!
```

## Grammar Assessment Prompts

### Tense Usage Assessment
```
**Grammar Check: Verb Tenses**

Complete these sentences with the correct form of the verb:

1. "I _____ (live) in this city for five years."
2. "When I arrived, they _____ already _____ (finish) the meeting."
3. "By next year, I _____ (complete) my studies."

*This helps me understand your grasp of different time expressions.*
```

### Complex Grammar Assessment
```
**Advanced Grammar Assessment**

Rewrite these sentences using the suggested structures:

1. "The weather is bad, so we can't go out." (Use conditional)
2. "She studied hard. She passed the exam." (Use a participle clause)
3. "People say that he is very talented." (Use passive voice)

*Don't worry if this seems challenging - it helps me gauge your level!*
```

## Vocabulary Assessment Prompts

### Word Knowledge Test
```
**Vocabulary Assessment**

Explain what these words mean or use them in a sentence:

{level_appropriate_words}

Example for beginners: happy, house, work, family
Example for intermediate: efficient, significant, appropriate, consequences

*Just try your best with the words you know.*
```

### Contextual Vocabulary
```
**Vocabulary in Context**

Read this sentence and explain what the highlighted word means:

"The company's *innovative* approach to marketing helped them gain a competitive advantage."

What does "innovative" mean in this context?
How would you use this word in your own sentence?

*This shows me how well you understand words in context.*
```
