# Intermediate Conversation Prompts

## Complex Discussion Topics

### Current Events and Society
- "What's your opinion on remote work? Do you think it's better than working in an office?"
- "How has technology changed the way we communicate?"
- "What environmental issues concern you the most?"

### Personal Growth and Goals
- "What's a skill you'd like to develop in the next year?"
- "How do you handle stress and pressure?"
- "What's the best piece of advice you've ever received?"

### Cultural Comparisons
- "How are holidays celebrated differently in various countries?"
- "What aspects of other cultures do you find most interesting?"
- "How has globalization affected your country?"

## Advanced Conversation Techniques

### Expressing Opinions
- "What's your take on...?"
- "How do you feel about the idea that...?"
- "Some people argue that... What's your perspective?"

### Hypothetical Situations
- "If you could change one thing about your city, what would it be?"
- "Imagine you had unlimited resources. What problem would you solve first?"
- "What would you do if you found out you had one year to live?"

### Abstract Concepts
- "What does success mean to you?"
- "How important is work-life balance in your culture?"
- "What role does tradition play in modern society?"

## Debate and Discussion Prompts

### Social Issues
- "Should social media platforms be responsible for fact-checking content?"
- "Is it better to specialize in one area or be a generalist?"
- "How much privacy should we sacrifice for security?"

### Lifestyle Choices
- "Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?"
- "Should people prioritize career or family?"
- "What's more important: financial security or personal fulfillment?"

## Language Practice Scenarios

### Professional Situations
- "Imagine you're presenting a new idea to your colleagues. How would you convince them?"
- "How would you handle a disagreement with a coworker?"
- "Describe your ideal work environment."

### Travel and Adventure
- "What's the most challenging travel experience you've had?"
- "How do you prefer to explore a new city?"
- "What's on your travel bucket list and why?"

## Advanced Grammar Practice

### Complex Tenses
- "Tell me about something you had been planning for a long time before it finally happened."
- "What will you have accomplished by the time you're 65?"
- "Describe a situation where you wish you had acted differently."

### Conditional Statements
- "If you hadn't learned this language, how would your life be different?"
- "What would happen if everyone in the world spoke the same language?"
- "If you could have dinner with anyone from history, who would it be and why?"

## Error Correction for Intermediate

### Complex Corrections
When student makes advanced errors:
"I can see you're working with complex grammar - that's excellent! Let me help you refine that. Instead of '[error]', try '[correction]'. This structure works better because..."

### Encouraging Advanced Attempts
- "I love that you're experimenting with complex sentences!"
- "Your vocabulary is really improving. Here's a more natural way to express that idea..."
- "You're thinking like an advanced speaker! Let me show you how native speakers typically phrase this..."
