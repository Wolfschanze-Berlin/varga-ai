# Beginner Conversation Prompts

## Daily Life Topics

### Morning Routine
- "Tell me about your morning routine. What do you do when you wake up?"
- "What did you have for breakfast today?"
- "What time do you usually get up in the morning?"

### Family and Friends  
- "Tell me about your family. How many people are in your family?"
- "Do you have any brothers or sisters?"
- "What does your best friend like to do?"

### Basic Preferences
- "What is your favorite color?"
- "Do you like cats or dogs better?"
- "What kind of music do you enjoy?"

### Simple Activities
- "What do you like to do in your free time?"
- "Do you watch TV? What shows do you like?"
- "Do you play any sports?"

## Conversation Starters by Level

### A1 Level (Complete Beginner)
- "Hello! What is your name?"
- "How are you today?"
- "Where are you from?"
- "What is your job?"
- "Do you have pets?"

### A2 Level (Elementary)
- "What did you do yesterday?"
- "What are your plans for the weekend?"
- "Tell me about your hometown."
- "What languages do you speak?"
- "What is your favorite season and why?"

## Vocabulary Building Prompts

### Basic Emotions
- "How are you feeling today? Are you happy, sad, tired, or excited?"
- "When do you feel most relaxed?"
- "What makes you angry?"

### Food and Eating
- "What is your favorite food?"
- "Do you like to cook?"
- "What did you eat for lunch today?"
- "Do you prefer sweet or salty snacks?"

### Home and Living
- "Describe your house or apartment."
- "What room do you spend most time in?"
- "Do you live alone or with family?"

## Error Correction Prompts

### Gentle Corrections
When student says: "I am go to work every day"
Response: "Great! I understand what you mean. A small correction: 'I go to work every day' or 'I am going to work today.' The simple present 'I go' is for routines. Keep practicing - you're doing well!"

### Encouraging Feedback
- "That's a good try! Let me help you with that..."
- "I can understand you perfectly! Here's a little tip to make it even better..."
- "Excellent effort! Native speakers make mistakes too. Here's how we usually say it..."

## Topic Transitions

### Natural Flow
- "That's interesting! Speaking of [topic], have you ever...?"
- "I'm curious about something else - do you...?"
- "Let's talk about something different. Tell me about..."

### Building on Responses
- "You mentioned [previous topic]. Can you tell me more about that?"
- "That reminds me of... What do you think about...?"
- "I'd love to know more about [detail they mentioned]."
